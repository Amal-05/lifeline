| 100 | 3,415 | 31.07 req/s | 695.40 ms | 2.34% | Thresholds failed |

At 100 VUs, most of the 80 failures were incident-read timeouts during saturation. These numbers describe this developer machine and demo dataset only. Raw k6 summaries are in `k6-summary-25vus.json` and `k6-summary-100vus.json`.
